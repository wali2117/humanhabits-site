Privacy Policy

Human Habits · Last updated: [DATE] · Effective: [DATE]

Human Habits is built on a simple premise: your habit data is yours, and we collect as little as possible to make the app work. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what we don't do with it.

1. Who we are

[Human Habits LLC], a Michigan limited liability company ("we," "us," or "our"), operates the Human Habits mobile application and the website humanhabits.app ("the Service"). Questions about this policy can be sent to support@humanhabits.app.

2. What we collect

Information you provide

  • Display name — only if you use the Friends feature. This is the only piece of information you actively provide. You choose what name to use; it does not need to be your real name.

Information generated automatically

  • Anonymous user ID — a random UUID generated on your device at first launch. It is never linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifying information.
  • Habit data — the habits you create, your completion logs, and your settings. This is stored locally on your device and, if you use cloud backup, synced to our servers in encrypted form under your anonymous ID.
  • Recovery key — a 24-character alphanumeric code generated when you enable backup. It is stored on our servers under your anonymous ID and is the only way to restore your data to a new device.
  • Crash reports — if the app crashes, Sentry (our crash reporting provider) collects technical information about the error, your device type, and operating system version. This data does not include your habit data or display name.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name (unless you provide a display name), email address, phone number, location, payment information, browsing history, or any biometric data. We have no advertising identifiers and use no ad networks.

3. How we use your information

  • To sync and restore your habit data across devices
  • To enable the Friends feature (display name only, visible only to accepted friends)
  • To diagnose and fix crashes via Sentry
  • To display aggregate, anonymized platform statistics (e.g., "X habits logged today") — no individual data is ever surfaced

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties. Ever.

4. How we store and protect your data

Your data is stored in Supabase, a PostgreSQL-based cloud platform with encryption at rest and in transit. Row-level security policies ensure that your data is only accessible by your anonymous session. We do not have a way to link your anonymous ID to your real identity.

5. Third-party services

  • Supabase — cloud database and authentication (anonymous). Privacy policy.
  • Sentry — crash reporting. Privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. RevenueCat may collect a device identifier to manage your subscription status. Privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing for subscriptions. We do not receive or store your payment details.

6. Data retention

Your data remains on our servers as long as your account is active. If you delete the app without using the recovery key, your anonymous account and its data will remain on our servers but is inaccessible to anyone, including us. You may request deletion of your data by contacting support@humanhabits.app with your recovery key, and we will delete your records within 30 days.

7. Children

Human Habits is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to request that we stop processing it. Because we collect almost no identifying information, most of these rights are satisfied by the nature of our architecture. For any request, contact support@humanhabits.app. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EU or UK, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you within the app. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
